What this is

We opened Invoice Maker the way a new user does, then rebuilt the parts we think are leaving money on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion and revenue across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Invoice Maker: installed it, went through every screen up to the home tab, read your App Store listing, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what you show people at the moment they decide.

What follows is five ideas — not fifteen. Each one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and each one is built into a clickable prototype so you can see it rather than imagine it. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Every screen in the prototype is yours — your serif headlines, your blue buttons, your invoice art, your wood-desk paywall and your Premium / Business switch. Mostly the order changed, plus a few things you already own but do not show at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history — and we know you run a lot of tests. If one of these is something you have already tried, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
