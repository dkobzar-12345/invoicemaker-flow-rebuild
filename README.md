# Growth Audit — Invoice Maker

**Live:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/invoicemaker-flow-rebuild/
**On a phone:** https://dkobzar-12345.github.io/invoicemaker-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

Five ranked ideas for Invoice Maker's iOS onboarding and paywall, plus a clickable prototype built in Invoice Maker's own design. Prepared by Adapty, November 2026.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Move sign-in behind the paywall | CR +8% · ARPU +17% |
| 02 | Ask what they need, and lead the paywall with it | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Offer Business to people who came to get paid | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Swap the rating request for your rating | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | One offer on close, on your own lower price | ARPU +10–15% |

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Invoice Maker.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, quote and feature line comes from Invoice Maker's own App Store listing and in-app screens.

**Run locally:** `python3 -m http.server 8000` in this folder, then open http://localhost:8000.
